Digital Humanities

This section features projects from my Digital Humanities course, where I combined research, technology, and storytelling. I developed a digital strategy for a museum, examined the cultural and ethical implications of A.I within literature, and created a Mercer Writers Project piece that tells the story of an influential Mercer figure.

Reflection

This was my first English course in college, and as a 391-level class, it challenged me more than courses I had taken before. While the material was difficult at times, I gained a strong understanding of key concepts, especially the importance of the humanities and the role of physical and digital spaces in preserving and sharing knowledge. I was particularly interested in discussions around AI’s impact on digital spaces, art, and the humanities, as well as the role of libraries and museums. I most enjoyed researching cultural institutions and designing a digital strategy for the Digital Cartography project. This course encouraged me to think more deeply about familiar ideas and articulate them in new ways.
