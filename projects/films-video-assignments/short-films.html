Short Films

The Making of: A Christmas Mourning

The Making of: A Christmas Mourning is a short comedic film that I created as a parody of “the making of” behind-the-scenes documentaries, created around a fictional holiday movie “A Christmas Mourning.” Using humor and mockumentary-style storytelling, the project playfully critiques production culture. This video was selected for inclusion in WMUB-TV Student Showcase.

Edited in Adobe Premiere Pro 2023

Don't Look at Me

Don’t Look at Me is a short film that portrays how anxiety can disrupt even the simplest moments, such as a walk to class. I created this film to raise awareness of mental health and turn an internal feeling into something viewers can see and empathize with.

Edited in Adobe Premiere Pro 2023

Do You See Me?

Do You See Me? is a short narrative film I created as part of a collaborative production project, where each member took on a specific role. For this piece, I served as the director, shaping the visual storytelling of a scene in which a girl enters a room and becomes increasingly disoriented by the other classmate in the room with her.

Edited in Adobe Premiere Pro 2024
