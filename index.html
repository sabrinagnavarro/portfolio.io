Mercer University Media Studies Graduate

I'm Sabrina Navarro, a 2025 Media Studies graduate with a minor in Political Science and Film Studies! I'm passionate about digital marketing, social media, and public relations. I enjoy creating meaningful, authentic content that helps brands connect with their audiences.

During my time with Sarah’s Social Strategy, I gained hands-on experience in social media and marketing while learning what goes on behind the scenes of building a strong digital presence. At Mercer University, I also worked as a Writing Preceptor, where I helped students strengthen their writing and provided guidance during class. That experience sharpened my communication skills and taught me how to adapt my approach to different people and perspectives.

I thrive in fast-paced environments, love learning new things, and bring a creative perspective to everything I do. And when I’m not working on a creative project, you can catch me looking for my next concert, coffee shop, or Atlanta event to check out!
